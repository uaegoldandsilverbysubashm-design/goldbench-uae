Per gram = (oz price × 3.6725 ÷ 31.1035) × karat ÷ 24 (24K = 100%, 22K = 91.67%, 21K = 87.5%, 18K = 75%). Gold value = tag gms × per gram. MC is AED per gram. VAT is charged on gold value + MC. You can overwrite any oz price by hand — it then stays as "manual price" until you change the date.
